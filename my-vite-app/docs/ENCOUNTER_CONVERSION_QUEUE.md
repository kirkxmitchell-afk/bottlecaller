## Recommended Batch Size
Do not convert everything at once.

Rhythm:
1. convert 3 encounters
2. playtest
3. fix friction
4. convert next 3

## Best Batch Structure
For each batch:
- 1 Decider-like encounter
- 1 Griever-like encounter
- 1 Fancy or Bargain Smart encounter

Why:
- stresses different psychologies
- catches drift earlier
- prevents repetitive authoring

## Conversion Selection Rule
Pick the next legacy encounter based on:
1. appears early in progression
2. uses one of the four core families
3. currently feels repetitive or weak
4. exposes one key skill focus cleanly

Tie-breaker: choose the one players see more often.

## Clean Stopping Rule (Definition of Migrated)
An encounter is migrated when:
- uses new variation schema
- has `A/B/C/D/E`
- difficulty gating works
- `pressureBias` and `trapProfile` are authored
- reflection stays clean
- no locked option leaks into feedback
- playtested at least once on Easy, Medium, Hard

This prevents fake completion.
